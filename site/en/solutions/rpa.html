Robotic process automation (RPA) with artificial intelligence

At Y&iF we use RPA and artificial intelligence to automate the repetitive tasks that take hours of your team's time today: data entry, reconciliations, reports and invoice handling. The bots work on top of your current systems, without replacing them, and log every step.

What is robotic process automation (RPA)?

Robotic process automation (RPA) uses software robots that repeat the actions a person performs on a computer today —copying data between systems, downloading files, filling in forms— accurately and at any hour. With artificial intelligence, they can also read documents, sort emails and resolve simple cases.

The most commonly automated processes

  • Downloading, validating and entering invoices (CFDI) into your accounting system or ERP.
  • Bank, accounts receivable and accounts payable reconciliations.
  • Order entry and onboarding of customers and suppliers.
  • Recurring reports that are built by hand in Excel today.
  • Data migration between systems that don't talk to each other.

Often the best solution combines both: the agent understands the case and the RPA bot carries out the steps in the systems.
