Custom software development

At Y&iF we build custom software for companies in Mexico and Latin America: web platforms, internal systems and integrations, with artificial intelligence where it adds value. We start from the business problem, validate before building and deliver in stages so you see progress from the start.

What we build

  • Web platforms and portals for customers, suppliers or partners.
  • Internal systems that replace spreadsheets and paper processes.
  • Integrations and APIs that connect your ERP, CRM and other tools.
  • AI features inside your software: smart search, assistants, data classification and extraction.
  • Prototypes and minimum viable products to validate an idea before overinvesting.
